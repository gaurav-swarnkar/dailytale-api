DailyTale Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

DailyTale is an app that tells the story of any day of the year: what happened, who was born, and what the day is celebrated as. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: you can use DailyTale without an account; if you create one, we keep only what is needed to sign you in, and you can delete it at any time.

Information we collect

If you create an account, we store:

  • your name and email address;
  • a user ID and the way you signed in (email and password, or Google);
  • your password, which is stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider and is never visible to us.

If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and Google account ID with DailyTale. We do not receive your Google password.

If you do not create an account, DailyTale does not collect any personal information about you.

How we use it

We use your account information only to let you sign in, keep you signed in on your device, verify your email address and help you reset a forgotten password. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except the service provider below.

Service providers

  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC) stores and manages accounts on our behalf. See Firebase privacy and security.

Content the app loads

The stories for each day are downloaded from our public data repository on GitHub, and story photos are loaded from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. As with any website, these servers see your device's IP address when the app requests a file. We do not send them any account information.

The narrator reads stories aloud using your phone's built-in text-to-speech. Nothing is recorded, and the app does not use your microphone, camera, location or contacts.

Analytics and ads

DailyTale contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Keeping your data and deleting it

We keep your account until you delete it. You can delete your account at any time in the app: tap the avatar at the top left, then Delete my account. This permanently removes your account record (name, email address, user ID and sign-in details) straight away. You can also ask us to delete it by email; see how to delete your DailyTale account.

Security

All information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored by Firebase Authentication, which encrypts data at rest.

Children

DailyTale is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
