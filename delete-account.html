Delete your DailyTale account

You can delete your DailyTale account and its data at any time, either in the app or by email.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open DailyTale and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap the avatar (your initial) at the top left of the home screen.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then Delete to confirm.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, sign out, sign back in, and repeat these steps. This is a security check for accounts that have been signed in for a long time.

By email

If you can no longer use the app, email CONTACT_EMAIL with the subject "Delete my DailyTale account", from the email address you registered with. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your name, email address, user ID and sign-in details (including any link to your Google account). DailyTale stores no other personal data, so nothing is kept afterwards.
